# Redfield Apocalypse

**Redfield Apocalypse** is a Zombie Apocalypse game mode, originally developed in 2018 and 2019.

In this gamemode, you face hordes of zombies, survive, and grow stronger. Here’s what you can do:  

- **Fight and survive:** Kill zombies to earn experience, money, and other rewards.  
- **Level up and progress:** Gain experience to unlock new weapons, features, and abilities.  
- **Expand your assets:** Buy vehicles and houses to improve your survival and mobility.  
- **Team up:** Form your own team with friends and share experience to grow stronger together.  
- **Customize and upgrade:** Collect bonus points to unlock skins, a weapon depot, and other enhancements.  
- **Safe zones:** Recharge and plan your strategy in areas protected from zombies.  
- **Premium benefits:** Activate Premium status to enjoy extra perks and advantages in the game.
